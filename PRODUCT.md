# Product

## Users

Mac users comparing software for a specific need, and developers presenting useful native applications to that audience.

## Product Purpose

Macify is a curated directory that helps people understand what a Mac application does, who it suits, what it costs, and what to know before installing it.

## Brand Personality

Curious, selective, and plain-spoken. The experience should feel recognizably Mac-native while retaining a playful editorial character.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic software-directory grids, ornamental glass effects, heavy shadows, excessive rounding, muted low-contrast prose, and decorative typography that makes long descriptions harder to read.

## Design Principles

- Make editorial judgment visible without overpowering measured rankings.
- Keep application information readable and concrete.
- Let distinctive display details frame the experience, not invade long-form prose.
- Prefer native performance and familiar interaction over unnecessary front-end weight.
- Preserve clear navigation, semantic structure, and honest disclosures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, comfortable reading measures, semantic page structure, keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and browser zoom compatibility.
